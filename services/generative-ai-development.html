Start with the job, not the model

The best AI projects begin with a task that is slow, repetitive or expensive today. We define what a good answer looks like, build a small version, test it on real examples and only then widen it.

How a support chatbot on your content works

Your pages and documents are split into passages and indexed. When a customer asks a question, the system finds the most relevant passages and the model writes an answer from them, with links back to the source. If it is not sure, it says so and passes the conversation to your team.

This approach is called retrieval-augmented generation. It keeps answers tied to what you have actually published.

A product we run ourselves

Chatish AI is our own AI support agent for websites. It learns your content, answers customers and hands over to a human when needed.

Questions about generative ai

How accurate will the AI be?

Accuracy depends on the quality of your content and how the system is set up. We test with real questions, show sources with every answer and set the AI to hand over when it is unsure.

Can the AI use our own documents?

Yes. That is the most common use. We index your website, help articles, PDFs and other documents so answers come from them.

How much does it cost to run?

There is a one-time build cost and a usage cost from the AI provider. We estimate the monthly usage up front and set limits so there are no surprises.

Is our data safe?

We review the provider's data terms with you and design around them. We will tell you honestly what is and is not suitable to send to an AI service.

Can it work on WhatsApp or our website?

Yes. We can place the assistant on your website, inside an app, or connect it to messaging channels.
