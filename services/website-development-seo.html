Why SEO belongs in the build

Most SEO problems are created during development: slow templates, missing headings, duplicate pages, or content that search engines cannot read. Fixing them later costs more than doing it properly the first time.

So we plan the page structure and the search terms each page should target before we design anything. Each page has one clear job and one clear topic.

Choosing the right platform

Next.js suits custom sites and web apps where speed and flexibility matter. Shopify is the practical choice for most online stores. WordPress works well when your team wants a familiar editor.

We will tell you which one fits, even when it is not the one we would enjoy building most.

Moving an existing site without losing traffic

If you already have a site that ranks, we map every old URL to a new one with 301 redirects, keep the titles and content that already perform, and watch Search Console after launch for anything that slips.

How long it takes

A marketing website usually takes two to four weeks. Online stores and larger sites take longer. You get a firm schedule after the first call.

Tools and technology

Next.js, React, Shopify, WordPress, Tailwind CSS, Vercel, Google Analytics 4, Google Search Console.

Questions about websites and seo

How much does a website cost?

It depends on the number of pages, the features and the platform. After a short call we give you a fixed-scope quote in USD, so you know the full price before we start.

Can you guarantee a number one ranking on Google?

No honest agency can. Rankings depend on competition, content quality and time. What we can do is build the site so nothing technical holds you back, then help with content and links to move you up.

Will I lose my rankings if you rebuild my current site?

Not if it is done carefully. We redirect every old URL, keep content that already performs and monitor Search Console after launch.

Who owns the website?

You do. Code, design files and accounts are handed over to you at the end of the project.

Do you work with US businesses?

Yes. Most of our clients are in the US. Our evenings in India are your mornings, so we hold calls at your 7 AM to noon and your updates are waiting when you wake up.
