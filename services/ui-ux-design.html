UI/UX design and branding

We design logos, colours and type first, then the screens. You click through a prototype before any code gets written, so changes are cheap and decisions are clear.

Book a free 30-minute callOr message us on WhatsApp

What is included

  • User flows and wireframes

    The steps people take, sketched out before any visual design.

  • UI design in Figma

    Polished screens for web and mobile, with all states designed.

  • Clickable prototypes

    A prototype you can open on your phone and test with real users.

  • Design systems

    Reusable colours, type and components so your product stays consistent as it grows.

  • Brand identity

    Logo, colour palette and typography that fit your business.

  • Developer hand-off

    Organised files and specs so what gets built matches what was designed.

Design that is easy to build

Because we also build websites and apps, we design with development in mind. That means fewer surprises, fewer revisions and a final product that looks like the design.

Prototype before you build

A clickable prototype shows how the product feels long before it is built. We review it with you, make changes and only then start development.

How long it takes

A brand identity usually takes two to three weeks. App or website design depends on the number of screens, and we confirm a schedule after the first call.

Tools and technology

Figma, FigJam, Lottie, Adobe Illustrator, design tokens, Storybook.

Questions about ui/ux and branding

What do I get at the end?

Final design files, a design system, a clickable prototype and an export of logos and brand assets in the formats you need.

Do you design only, or build too?

Both. You can hire us for design alone, or we can carry on and build it.

How many revisions are included?

We agree the number of review rounds in the written scope so everyone knows what to expect.

Can you redesign my existing app or site?

Yes. We start by reviewing what works and what does not, then redesign from there.
