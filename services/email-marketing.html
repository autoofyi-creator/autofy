Deliverability first

The best email is worthless if it lands in spam. Before sending, we authenticate your domain with SPF, DKIM and DMARC and check your list quality. Then we warm up sending gradually.

Flows do the heavy lifting

A handful of automated flows usually produce more revenue than one-off campaigns. We build those first, then add regular newsletters on top.

Permission matters

We only email people who have agreed to hear from you, and we follow the rules that apply in your market, including CAN-SPAM in the US. We do not buy or rent lists.

How long it takes

A core set of flows can usually be built and live within two to four weeks.

Tools and technology

Klaviyo, Mailchimp, Shopify Email, Brevo, Google Postmaster Tools, SPF, DKIM, DMARC.

Questions about email marketing

Klaviyo or Mailchimp?

Klaviyo is usually stronger for online stores. Mailchimp is simpler for newsletters and small lists. We recommend the one that fits your tools and budget.

Can you do cold outreach?

We can set up outreach to business contacts where the law allows, with proper authentication and careful volume. We will not send to purchased lists.

How many emails should I send?

Start with a few automated flows and one or two newsletters a month. We adjust based on opens, clicks and unsubscribes.

Can you write the emails too?

Yes. We write and design them, and you approve before anything is sent.

Will you work in our time zone?

We hold calls in your morning and keep campaigns scheduled for your customers' local time.
