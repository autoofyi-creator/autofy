One codebase or two?

For most first versions, a single cross-platform codebase gets you on both stores faster and costs less to maintain. We choose Flutter or React Native depending on the product.

If your app depends heavily on the camera, Bluetooth, background tasks or very smooth animation, going native with Kotlin and Swift can be the better call. We will explain the trade-off in plain terms.

Questions about android and ios apps

How much does it cost to build an app?

It depends on the features, the platforms and the backend. We give a fixed-scope quote in USD after a short call. A smaller first version is a good way to start and learn.

Do you build for both Android and iOS?

Yes. We build both, usually from one codebase. The demo downloads on our site are Android files, and we can send you a TestFlight invite to try an iOS build.

Will you publish the app for me?

Yes. We prepare the listings, screenshots and privacy details, submit the app and deal with the review process.

Who owns the code?

You do. The source code and the developer accounts are yours.

Can you add features to an existing app?

Yes. We can take over an existing Flutter, React Native or native codebase after a short review of how it is built.
