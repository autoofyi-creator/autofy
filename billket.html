Billket: GST billing app for small businesses

Billket helps shops, wholesalers and distributors create GST invoices, manage stock and track payments from an Android phone. It is live on Google Play and was built by Autofy.

Get it on Google PlayWant a custom app like this? Book a call

What Billket does

  • Invoices and sales

    Create sale invoices quickly and keep every bill in one place.

  • Purchases and orders

    Record purchases, sell orders and purchase orders.

  • Quotations and challans

    Send quotations and delivery challans from the same app.

  • Payments in and out

    Track money you are owed and money you have to pay.

  • Items and stock

    Keep your item list and stock in order.

  • Expenses

    Log rent, staff salary, maintenance and custom expenses.

  • Parties and reports

    See customers and suppliers, and reports on how the business is doing.

  • GST support

    Built for businesses that bill with GST in India.

Questions about Billket

What is Billket?

Billket is a billing and invoicing app for small businesses in India. It helps you create GST invoices, manage items and stock, record sales and purchases, and keep track of payments.

Where can I get Billket?

Billket is available on Google Play for Android.

Who is Billket for?

Shops, wholesalers, distributors and other small businesses that need simple billing and stock tracking.

Who built Billket?

Billket was built by Autofy, a software studio in Tripura, India. We can build a similar custom app for your business.
