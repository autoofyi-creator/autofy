( 06 ) Good questions

Before you ask.

Do you work with clients in the US?

Yes, that’s most of our work. We schedule calls in your morning, keep a shared channel on Slack or email, and send short video updates so nothing waits for a time zone.

How does pricing work?

Projects get a fixed-scope quote in USD with written milestones. Ongoing work like SEO, ads and email runs on a simple monthly retainer you can pause or change.

Who owns the code and designs?

You do. Source code, design files, accounts and data are transferred to you on final payment. We can sign an NDA before we talk details.

How long does a project take?

A marketing website is typically 2–4 weeks. Mobile apps usually take 8–16 weeks depending on scope. AI integrations can often ship in a few weeks. We’ll give you a firm timeline after the first call.

Do you build iOS as well as Android?

Yes. Our demo downloads are Android APKs, but we build for the App Store too. Ask us for a TestFlight invite to try an iOS build.

Can you help after launch?

That’s where a lot of the value is. We handle maintenance, SEO, campaigns and email so your product keeps growing after the launch day.
