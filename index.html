What we do

Most clients start with one thing, a website or an app, and add the rest as they grow. Everything below is done in-house.

Websites and SEO

A site that loads fast, reads clearly and gets found on Google. We build on Next.js, Shopify or WordPress, depending on what you’ll need to edit yourself later.

You get design, development, on-page and technical SEO, schema markup, speed work, and analytics that are set up properly.

Android and iOS apps

One codebase with Flutter or React Native when that makes sense, native Kotlin or Swift when it doesn’t. We also handle the store listings and the review process.

You get a prototype, the app, the backend and admin panel, and submission to Google Play and the App Store.

Generative AI

Chatbots trained on your own content, tools that read documents and fill in forms, and AI features added to software you already use. We pick the model that fits the job and the budget.

You get AI support chat, search over your own data, workflow automation, and integrations with OpenAI, Claude or Gemini.

Digital marketing

Google and Meta ads, local SEO and content, with tracking set up so you can see which spend actually brings in customers.

You get campaign setup and management, conversion tracking, and a monthly report you can read in five minutes.

Email marketing

Welcome emails, abandoned-cart flows, newsletters and outreach. We sort out your sending domain and deliverability first, so the emails reach inboxes.

You get templates, automated flows in Klaviyo or Mailchimp, list setup, and ongoing campaigns.

UI/UX and branding

Logo, colours and type first, then the screens. You click through a prototype before any code gets written.

You get brand identity, a design system, and clickable prototypes.

How a project goes

No long proposals and no jargon. Here’s what to expect from the first message to launch.

  1. Day 1

    A short call

    Thirty minutes, free. You tell us what you need. We ask a lot of questions and tell you honestly if we’re not the right fit.

  2. Days 2–3

    A written quote

    You get the scope, a fixed price in USD and a timeline, in writing. If something changes later, we talk about it before it costs you anything.

  3. Build

    Design and development

    You see a working version every week, on a link you can open on your phone. Feedback is welcome early, when it’s cheap to act on.

  4. Launch

    Go live, then grow

    We launch it and hand over everything, including code, files and accounts. If you want, we stay on for SEO, ads and email.

Questions people ask

How does pricing work?

Projects get a fixed quote in USD with written milestones. Ongoing work like SEO, ads and email is a simple monthly retainer that you can pause or change.

Who owns the code and designs?

You do. Source code, design files, accounts and data are handed over to you at the end. We’re happy to sign an NDA before you share details.

How long does a project take?

A marketing website is usually two to four weeks. Mobile apps take eight to sixteen weeks, depending on size. AI features can often ship in a few weeks. You’ll get a firm timeline after the first call.

Do you build for iPhone as well as Android?

Yes. The demo downloads on this page are Android files, but we build for the App Store too. Message us for a TestFlight invite to try an iOS build.

Do you keep working after launch?

If you want us to. Many clients keep us on for maintenance, SEO, ads and email. Others take the project and run it themselves. Both are fine.

Can I try your work before I hire you?

That’s why the demo apps are on this page. Install them, click around, and ask us anything about how they were built.
